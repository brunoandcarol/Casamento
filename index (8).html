Welcome

From Berlin to the beach

As some of you already know (and some of you were even there!), we got married in Berlin on 27 March 2026. But it has always been our dream to bring our friends, our cultures and a party together in Brazil, Bruno's home country and the place Carol has taken to her heart. Now that dream is coming true!

We are so happy to welcome you to Itacoatiara, a place that has been part of so many moments in Bruno's life and that Carol has fallen in love with too. Come and dance with us on a night that brings all of this together.

Questions

Where should I stay?

Itacoatiara itself is small. Nearby Itaipu, Camboinhas and Piratininga have more places to stay. Book early: January is peak season in Brazil.

What is the best way to get there?

By Uber or taxi: buses do not go into Itacoatiara.
